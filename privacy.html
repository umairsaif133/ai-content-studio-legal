AI Content Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

What this app is

AI Content Studio is a video editing application that runs on the user's own computer. It is used by its owner to create videos and publish them to the owner's own social media accounts.

What information is used

When you connect an account (for example TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Instagram or YouTube), the app receives from that platform an access token and the account's display name. It uses them only to show which account is connected and to publish the videos you choose to post.

Where it is stored

Access tokens, your videos and your project files are stored only on your own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the developer, and no analytics or tracking are used.

Sharing

Information is sent to a social platform only when you ask the app to publish a video to that platform, and only to the account you connected. The app does not sell or share personal data with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect any account inside the app at any time, which deletes its stored token from your computer. You can also revoke the app's access from the settings of the platform concerned.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
