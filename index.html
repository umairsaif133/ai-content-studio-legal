AI Content Studio

AI Content Studio is a video production app that runs on the owner's own computer. It combines camera and screen recording, a multi-track timeline, captions and rendering, and lets the owner publish finished videos to their own social media accounts, such as TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and Pinterest.

Videos, project files and sign-in tokens stay on the user's computer. Nothing is uploaded to the developer's servers.
